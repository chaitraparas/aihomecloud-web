Where can this rank get a clinical seat?

Every clinical and diploma seat that went to an Open or EWS candidate in the 2025 counselling rounds: MCC All-India (Rounds 1–3 and stray) and Maharashtra State CET Cell (Rounds 1–3, stray and special vacancy). A seat counts as reachable when its 2025 closing rank was at or beyond yours.

Reading this

  • 2025 is a proxy. Closing ranks move each year with seat counts and cutoffs. Treat the chance labels as bands, not promises.
  • Late rounds are leftovers. In 2025 the qualifying cutoff was lowered before Round 3, which pulled in many lower ranks. Seats that only open late are often costly or less sought after.
  • Government clinical seats are rare at ranks around 50,000. Government non-clinical seats (Biochemistry, Physiology, Anatomy, Microbiology, Forensic, Pharmacology) are available: over 1,000 went to lower Open ranks in Rounds 1–2, and EWS adds about 300 more. They are not listed here.

Not in the data

  • Fees. They decide most deemed and institutional-quota choices. Check each college's fee notice.
  • Govt vs private in Maharashtra is inferred: colleges with institutional or NRI seats are marked private. DNY Tasgaon Karjat is probably private despite its label.
  • EWS seats need a valid EWS certificate at reporting: the central format for All-India (MCC) seats and the Maharashtra format for state seats. Check the 2026 brochures for the income year it must cover. Deemed-university seats have no EWS reservation.
  • Excluded: university-internal quotas (DU, IP, AMU, BHU), NRI, minority and armed-forces seats, and other states' domicile quotas.
