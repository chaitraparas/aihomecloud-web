Word Rush Support

Word Rush — Offline Word Party Game

How do I start a game?

Enter a name and tap Host. Everyone else opens Word Rush on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot and taps Join — the game appears in the list, or they can scan the QR code shown on the host's screen.

Other phones can't find my game

Make sure: (1) every phone is on the same Wi-Fi network or connected to the same hotspot, (2) the host phone stays on the lobby screen with its display on, (3) the Wi-Fi is not a guest network that blocks devices from seeing each other. If the game still doesn't appear, join by scanning the QR code or typing the address shown under "others join at" on the host's screen.

Do I need internet?

No. Word Rush only uses your local network. A phone hotspot with mobile data switched off works fine.

Why does the app ask for the camera?

Only to scan the host's QR code when joining a game. You can decline and join by typing the address instead.

Can I play alone?

Yes — Practice mode lets you play solo and beat your personal best.

How do I clear my game history?

Open the History screen and use the clear option. History is stored only on your phone.
