Other things we've built.

AiHomeCloud is the main project, but four other independent apps came out of solving our own problems first. Same philosophy on all of them: private by default, no subscription traps, built by people who actually use them.

FinPath

A FIRE (financial independence) and net-worth tracking app. Model SIP projections, plan an early-retirement date, and track your net worth over time — entirely on-device. No account, no cloud, no ads.

FinanceFIREAndroid
Play Store → Privacy Support

AHC Player

A private, Netflix-style media player for Android TV and Fire TV. Streams from an AiHomeCloud server, SMB shares, or local/USB storage — with posters, backdrops, and continue-watching, all on your own network.

MediaAndroid TVSelf-hosted
Privacy Support

WaterTank

Automates a home water pump over Bluetooth via an ESP32. Detects when supply arrives, starts the motor, and stops it when the tank is full — no Wi-Fi required.

IoTHomeAndroid
Play Store → Privacy Support

Word Rush

A fast word party game for everyone in the room. One phone hosts, the others join over local Wi-Fi or by scanning a QR code, and everyone races to make a word from the same letters — no internet, no accounts, no ads.
