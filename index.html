What actually happens

Every security promise, in both languages.

Every security promise, twice: once for the family that wants it to work, once for the homelab that wants to verify.

Your phone
Plain wordsFinds itself. On home Wi-Fi, the phone locates your server automatically — nothing to type, nothing to configure.mDNS on your LAN — service _aihomecloud._tcp.local
Plain wordsTrust, once. Your phone learns your server's identity the first time they meet, and refuses impostors after that.TLS 1.3 · trust-on-first-use certificate pinning
Plain wordsIdentity can't be faked. Even if someone broke into the server software, they still couldn't pretend to be your server.Certificate rotation signed by a root-owned process the network-facing API can never touch
Your server
Plain wordsSearch stays home. Type "diwali sparklers" and find the photo — the understanding happens inside your house.On-device semantic embedding model · zero egress
Plain wordsNothing to breach. There are no AiHomeCloud storage servers holding your life — nothing to leak, nothing to mine.Zero-retention architecture · media and embeddings never transit a third-party server
Your disk
0 bytes of your data stored on AiHomeCloud's servers. We couldn't sell, leak, or lose your files even if we wanted to — we don't have them.

Ownership

"Anyone can build an app with AI now. Why pay for this one?"

FROM THE PEOPLE WHO BUILD IT — NO SCRIPT, NO FUNNEL.

A fair question, and honestly, the first version did come together fast. What that skips: the first version was maybe 20% of the real work.

AiHomeCloud took about a year, start to finish — writing the specs, designing around real families, testing on real hardware bought with our own money, and more review cycles than we'll admit. Well over $1,000 went to AI subscriptions along the way, building this — AI sped the typing up; it didn't do the thinking, the testing, or the fixing.

You never have to pay. The free tier is the complete product for one person, and it will stay that way. But if AiHomeCloud earns its place in your home, Plus is a genuine way to keep it alive and improving.

— Chaitra Paras
